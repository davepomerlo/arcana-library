::: note About the text
The *Kojiki* 古事記 (“Record of Ancient Matters”) is written in Chinese characters, but much of it is meant to be read in Japanese. Where Yasumaro needed a Japanese word that Chinese could not express, he spelled it out phonetically with characters chosen for their sound. Two examples below are the drifting “jellyfish” clause, 久羅下那州多陀用弊流 *kurage nasu tadayoeru*, and the name 宇摩志阿斯訶備比古遲 *Umashi-ashikabi-hikoji*.
:::

## The first five kami: the Separate Heavenly Kami 別天神

::: parallel 1
天地初發之時、於高天原成神名、天之御中主神【訓高下天云阿麻。下效此】、次高御產巢日神、次神產巢日神。此三柱神者、並獨神成坐而、隱身也。
---
ametsuchi hajimete okorishi toki ni, Takaamanohara ni nareru kami no na wa, Ame-no-Minakanushi-no-kami. tsugi ni Takamimusuhi-no-kami. tsugi ni Kamumusuhi-no-kami. kono mihashira no kami wa, mina hitorigami to narimashite, mi wo kakushitamaiki.
---
When Heaven and Earth first became active, the kami who came into being on the High Plain of Heaven, 高天原 Takaamanohara, were named Ame-no-Minakanushi-no-kami 天之御中主神, “Lord of the August Centre of Heaven”; next, Takamimusuhi-no-kami 高御產巢日神, “High August Generating Power”; next, Kamumusuhi-no-kami 神產巢日神, “Divine Generating Power”. These three kami all came into being as solitary kami, 獨神 hitorigami, without partners, and hid their bodies.
[Gloss: read 高 followed by 天 as *ama*; likewise below.]
:::

::: parallel 2
次國稚如浮脂而、久羅下那州多陀用弊流之時【流字以上十字以音】、如葦牙因萌騰之物而成神名、宇摩志阿斯訶備比古遲神【此神名以音】、次天之常立神【訓常云登許、訓立云多知】。此二柱神亦、獨神成坐而、隱身也。
---
tsugi ni kuni wakaku uki-abura no gotoku shite, kurage nasu tadayoeru toki, ashikabi no gotoku moeagaru mono ni yorite nareru kami no na wa, Umashi-ashikabi-hikoji-no-kami. tsugi ni Ame-no-Tokotachi-no-kami. kono futahashira no kami mo mata, hitorigami to narimashite, mi wo kakushitamaiki.
---
Next, when the land was young, like floating oil, and drifted like a jellyfish (*kurage nasu tadayoeru*), there came into being, from something that sprouted up like reed shoots, 葦牙 ashikabi, the kami named Umashi-ashikabi-hikoji-no-kami 宇摩志阿斯訶備比古遲神, “Excellent Reed-Shoot Elder”; next, Ame-no-Tokotachi-no-kami 天之常立神, “Eternal Standing of Heaven”. These two kami also came into being as solitary kami and hid their bodies.
[Glosses: the ten characters ending with 流 are read for their sound; this kami's name is read for its sound; read 常 as *toko* and 立 as *tachi*.]
:::

::: parallel 3
上件五柱神者、別天神。
---
kami no kudari no itsuhashira no kami wa, kotoamatsukami.
---
The five kami above are the Separate Heavenly Kami, 別天神 kotoamatsukami.
:::

## The Seven Generations of the Age of the Kami 神世七代

::: parallel 4
次成神名、國之常立神、次豐雲野神。此二柱神亦、獨神成坐而、隱身也。
---
tsugi ni nareru kami no na wa, Kuni-no-Tokotachi-no-kami, tsugi ni Toyokumono-no-kami. kono futahashira no kami mo mata, hitorigami to narimashite, mi wo kakushitamaiki.
---
The kami who came into being next were named Kuni-no-Tokotachi-no-kami 國之常立神, “Eternal Standing of the Land”, and next Toyokumono-no-kami 豐雲野神, “Abundant Cloud-Moor”. These two kami also came into being as solitary kami and hid their bodies.
:::

::: parallel 5
次成神名、宇比地邇神、次妹須比智邇神。次角杙神、次妹活杙神。次意富斗能地神、次妹大斗乃辨神。次於母陀流神、次妹阿夜訶志古泥神。次伊邪那岐神、次妹伊邪那美神。
---
tsugi ni nareru kami no na wa, Uhijini-no-kami, tsugi ni imo Suhijini-no-kami. tsugi ni Tsunuguhi-no-kami, tsugi ni imo Ikuguhi-no-kami. tsugi ni Ōtonoji-no-kami, tsugi ni imo Ōtonobe-no-kami. tsugi ni Omodaru-no-kami, tsugi ni imo Ayakashikone-no-kami. tsugi ni Izanaki-no-kami, tsugi ni imo Izanami-no-kami.
---
The kami who came into being next were named Uhijini-no-kami 宇比地邇神, “Mud Lord”, and next his younger sister, 妹 imo, Suhijini-no-kami 須比智邇神, “Sand Lady”;
next Tsunuguhi-no-kami 角杙神, “Horn-Stake”, and next his sister Ikuguhi-no-kami 活杙神, “Living Stake”;
next Ōtonoji-no-kami 意富斗能地神, “Elder of the Great Place”, and next his sister Ōtonobe-no-kami 大斗乃辨神, “Lady of the Great Place”;
next Omodaru-no-kami 於母陀流神, “Perfect Face”, and next his sister Ayakashikone-no-kami 阿夜訶志古泥神, “Ah, How Awesome!”;
next Izanaki-no-kami 伊邪那岐神, “He Who Invites”, and next his sister Izanami-no-kami 伊邪那美神, “She Who Invites”.
:::

::: parallel 6
上件自國之常立神以下、伊邪那美神以前、并稱神世七代。【上二柱獨神、各云一代。次雙十神、各合二神云一代也。】
---
kami no kudari no Kuni-no-Tokotachi-no-kami yori shimo, Izanami-no-kami made, awasete kamiyo nanayo to mōsu.
---
The kami listed above, from Kuni-no-Tokotachi-no-kami down to Izanami-no-kami, are together called the Seven Generations of the Age of the Kami, 神世七代 kamiyo nanayo.
[Gloss: the first two solitary kami each count as one generation; of the ten paired kami that follow, each pair counts as one generation.]
:::

::: note Translation notes
**神 *kami*** is left untranslated. Kami are not gods in the Greek or biblical sense: they are any presence felt to be awe-inspiring or extraordinary.

**妹 *imo*** literally means “younger sister”. Here it marks the female partner of each pair. In Old Japanese *imo* was also a word for a beloved woman or wife.

**The readings** (romanization layer) follow the conventional modern *kundoku* rendering, which is itself a reconstruction. Motoori Norinaga's eighteenth-century *Kojiki-den* 古事記伝 shaped most later readings. The names Izanaki and Izanagi are both current; *Izanaki* is closer to the Old Japanese.

**The glosses on the names** are approximate. The meanings of several of these names are uncertain or disputed.
:::
