::: note About this text
The *Kumulipo* ("Beginning in deep darkness") is a *koʻihonua*, a genealogical chant, composed to honour the birth of a chief and to tie his line to the origin of everything. It was kept orally for generations. King Kalākaua had it printed in 1889, and his sister, Queen Liliʻuokalani, translated it into English in 1895–97 while under house arrest after the overthrow of the Hawaiian monarchy.

Its 16 sections (*wā*) move in order: the first seven in Pō, the night, tell the birth of sea creatures, plants, insects, birds and animals in pairs, each land creature matched with one in the sea; from the eighth onward, in Ao, the daylight world, the chant tells of gods and then of human generations down to the chief. Scholars have often compared its order of creation, from the coral polyp upward, to evolution.
:::

## The first wā

::: parallel 1
O ke au i kahuli wela ka honua
O ke au i kahuli lole ka lani
O ke au i kuka'iaka ka la
E ho'omalamalama i ka malama
O ke au o Makali'i ka po
---
At the time when the earth turned hot,
at the time when the heavens turned about,
at the time when the sun was in shadow,
to let the moon give light,
the time of Makaliʻi, the Pleiades, in the night:
:::

::: parallel 2
O ka walewale ho'okumu honua ia
O ke kumu o ka lipo, i lipo ai
O ke kumu o ka Po, i po ai
O ka lipolipo, o ka lipolipo
O ka lipo o ka la, o ka lipo o ka po
Po wale ho--'i
---
the slime, that was the source of the earth;
the source of the deep darkness, that made the darkness deep;
the source of the Night, that made it night;
the deepest darkness, the deepest darkness,
the darkness of the sun, the darkness of the night:
nothing but night.
:::

::: parallel 3
Hanau ka po
Hanau Kumulipo i ka po, he kane
Hanau Po'ele i ka po, he wahine
---
The night gave birth.
Born was Kumulipo in the night, a male;
born was Pōʻele in the night, a female.
:::

::: note Words worth knowing
- ***Pō***: night, darkness, and also the realm of the gods and the dead, the source from which all life comes. The chant repeats it until it becomes a drumbeat.
- ***kumu***: source, root, foundation, teacher. *Kumulipo* is "the source of deep darkness" and *Pōʻele* "black night".
- ***Makaliʻi***: the Pleiades. Their rising after sunset in late autumn opened the Makahiki season in old Hawaiʻi.
- ***walewale***: slime, ooze. Life begins in the sea-slime before the coral polyp, the first creature born in the next lines.
- ***Po wale hoʻi***: the long dash in the printed text marks a held, drawn-out vowel in performance.
:::
