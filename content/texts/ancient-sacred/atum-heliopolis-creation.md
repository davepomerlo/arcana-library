::: note Notice: what is original here and what is not
Egyptian was written in hieroglyphs, which this site does not yet display, so the original appears as a **transliteration**: the standard Egyptological way of writing the consonants in Latin letters (vowels were not written and are mostly unknown). The PT 527 lines follow the Thesaurus Linguae Aegyptiae, the main scholarly database of Egyptian texts, as visible in its public search results; we could not open the full pages to check every sign. The other two passages are given **in translation only**, as clearly marked. One word in PT 527 is uncertain: the TLA analyses it as *jwi̯ sꜣwi̯*, while most translators read a single word *jwsꜣw*, "the one who masturbated".
:::

::: note About these texts
From about 2350 BCE, the walls of the burial chambers of kings and queens at Saqqara were covered with spells to help the dead ruler rise to the sky: the **Pyramid Texts**, the oldest large body of religious writing anywhere. They do not tell a creation story from beginning to end, but they mention it again and again, because the king hoped to join the creator.

The creator of the Heliopolis tradition is **Atum**, "the Complete One" or "the Not-yet-existing", who emerges alone from the dark water (**Nun**) onto the first mound of land. With no partner, he produces **Shu** (air, emptiness) and **Tefnut** (moisture), who in turn give birth to **Geb** (earth) and **Nut** (sky). Their children Osiris, Isis, Seth and Nephthys complete the **Ennead**, the nine gods of Heliopolis.
:::

## Pyramid Text 527: Atum and his twins

::: parallel 1
ḏd-mdw
(J)tm(.w) ḫpr m jwi̯ sꜣwi̯ jr =f m Jwn.w
---
Words to be spoken:
Atum is the one who came into being, who masturbated (?) in Heliopolis.
:::

::: parallel 2
wdi̯.n =f ḥnn =f m ḫfꜥ =f
jri̯ =f nḏmm.t jm =f
msi̯ zꜣ.t.DU sn.t(j) Šw ḥnꜥ Tfn.t
---
He put his phallus in his fist
to make pleasure with it,
and the twin children were born, Shu and Tefnut.
:::

The utterance ends by asking the twins to place the king between them, among the gods, so that he may rise to the sky and come down to the earth "for life everlasting".

## Pyramid Text 600: the hill and the benben (translation only)

> O Atum-Khepri! You rose up as the hill; you shone forth as the *benben* stone in the House of the Phoenix in Heliopolis. You spat out Shu, you coughed out Tefnut, and you put your arms around them as the arms of a *ka*, so that your *ka* might be in them.

*Our translation, compared with Mercer (1952) and the German of the Thesaurus Linguae Aegyptiae. Here creation is by spitting: the verbs* jšš *and* tf *are puns on the names Shu and Tefnut.*

## The Bremner-Rhind papyrus: the creator speaks (translation only)

About two thousand years later, a priest copied a longer account into a book of spells against the serpent Apophis (British Museum EA 10188, dated 311 BCE). It is titled the **Book of Knowing the Evolutions (*ḫprw*) of Ra**, and the creator, called **Neb-er-djer**, "Lord to the Limit", tells it himself:

> I am the one who came into being as Khepri. When I came into being, Being came into being, and all beings came into being after I came into being. Many were the beings that came forth from my mouth. The sky had not yet come into being, the earth had not come into being... I was alone; I found no place where I could stand.

*Our paraphrase, following Budge (1912) and Faulkner's later translation (1938). The text plays continuously on the root* ḫpr, *"to come into being", which is also the name of Khepri, the scarab beetle who rolls the sun.*

In the same text, the creator's Eye goes out to find Shu and Tefnut in the darkness; when it brings them back, he weeps, and from his tears (*rmyt*) humankind (*rmṯ*) comes into being.

::: note Words worth knowing
- **ḫpr** (*kheper*): "to come into being, to transform". The scarab beetle, *ḫprr*, seemed to be born from nothing out of a ball of dung, so it became the image of self-creation and of the sunrise.
- **benben**: the sacred stone of Heliopolis, shaped like the first mound rising from the water. The pointed tops of pyramids and obelisks imitate it.
- **ka**: the life force passed from parent to child. Atum embracing his children "as the arms of a *ka*" is the hieroglyph of two raised arms.
- **Jwn.w** (*Iunu*): Heliopolis, "Sun City", now part of north-east Cairo.
:::
