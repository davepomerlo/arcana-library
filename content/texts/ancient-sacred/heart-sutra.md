## The sutra

::: parallel 1
仏説摩訶般若波羅蜜多心経
---
Bussetsu ma-ka han-nya ha-ra-mit-ta shin-gyō
---
The Heart Sutra of the Great Perfection of Wisdom, spoken by the Buddha
:::

::: parallel 2
観自在菩薩
行深般若波羅蜜多時
照見五蘊皆空
度一切苦厄
---
Kan-ji-zai bo-sa
gyō jin han-nya ha-ra-mit-ta ji
shō-ken go-on kai-kū
do is-sai ku-yaku
---
The Bodhisattva Kanjizai 観自在 (Avalokiteshvara),
while practising the deep perfection of wisdom,
saw clearly that the five aggregates 五蘊 (form, feeling, perception, formations, consciousness) are all empty,
and passed beyond all suffering and distress.
:::

::: parallel 3
舎利子
色不異空　空不異色
色即是空　空即是色
受想行識　亦復如是
---
Sha-ri-shi
shiki fu-i kū, kū fu-i shiki,
shiki soku-ze kū, kū soku-ze shiki,
ju sō gyō shiki, yaku-bu nyo-ze
---
Sharishi 舎利子 (Shariputra):
form is not different from emptiness; emptiness is not different from form.
Form is emptiness; emptiness is form.
So too are feeling, perception, formations and consciousness.
:::

::: parallel 4
舎利子
是諸法空相
不生不滅　不垢不浄　不増不減
---
Sha-ri-shi
ze sho-hō kū-sō
fu-shō fu-metsu, fu-ku fu-jō, fu-zō fu-gen
---
Sharishi: all dharmas 諸法 (phenomena) are marked by emptiness.
They are not born and do not perish, are not defiled and not pure, do not increase and do not decrease.
:::

::: parallel 5
是故空中
無色　無受想行識
無眼耳鼻舌身意
無色声香味触法
無眼界　乃至無意識界
---
ze-ko kū-chū
mu-shiki, mu ju sō gyō shiki,
mu gen ni bi zes-shin i,
mu shiki shō kō mi soku hō,
mu gen-kai, nai-shi mu i-shiki-kai
---
Therefore in emptiness
there is no form, no feeling, perception, formations or consciousness;
no eye, ear, nose, tongue, body or mind;
no colour, sound, smell, taste, touch or object of mind;
no realm of the eye, and so on up to no realm of mind-consciousness.
:::

::: parallel 6
無無明　亦無無明尽
乃至無老死　亦無老死尽
無苦集滅道
無智亦無得
---
mu mu-myō, yaku mu mu-myō-jin,
nai-shi mu rō-shi, yaku mu rō-shi-jin,
mu ku shū metsu dō,
mu-chi yaku mu-toku
---
There is no ignorance and no ending of ignorance,
and so on up to no old age and death and no ending of old age and death.
There is no suffering, no cause of suffering, no cessation, no path.
There is no wisdom and no attainment.
:::

::: parallel 7
以無所得故
菩提薩埵
依般若波羅蜜多故
心無罣礙　無罣礙故
無有恐怖
遠離一切顛倒夢想
究竟涅槃
---
i mu-sho-tok-ko
bo-dai-sat-ta
e han-nya ha-ra-mit-ta ko
shin mu-kei-ge, mu-kei-ge ko
mu-u ku-fu
on-ri is-sai ten-dō mu-sō
ku-gyō ne-han
---
Because there is nothing to attain,
the bodhisattva 菩提薩埵,
relying on the perfection of wisdom,
has a mind without hindrance. Without hindrance,
there is no fear.
Far beyond all upside-down, dreamlike thinking,
he reaches final nirvana 涅槃.
:::

::: parallel 8
三世諸仏
依般若波羅蜜多故
得阿耨多羅三藐三菩提
---
san-ze sho-butsu
e han-nya ha-ra-mit-ta ko
toku a-noku-ta-ra san-myaku san-bo-dai
---
All the Buddhas of the three times (past, present and future),
relying on the perfection of wisdom,
attain *anuttarā samyak-saṃbodhi* 阿耨多羅三藐三菩提, unsurpassed complete awakening.
:::

::: parallel 9
故知般若波羅蜜多
是大神呪　是大明呪
是無上呪　是無等等呪
能除一切苦
真実不虚
---
ko-chi han-nya ha-ra-mit-ta
ze dai-jin-shu, ze dai-myō-shu,
ze mu-jō-shu, ze mu-tō-dō-shu,
nō-jo is-sai-ku,
shin-jitsu fu-ko
---
So know that the perfection of wisdom
is the great divine mantra, the great bright mantra,
the unsurpassed mantra, the mantra equal to the unequalled,
which removes all suffering.
It is true and not false.
:::

::: parallel 10
故説般若波羅蜜多呪
即説呪曰
羯諦羯諦　波羅羯諦
波羅僧羯諦　菩提薩婆訶
般若心経
---
ko-setsu han-nya ha-ra-mit-ta shu
soku-setsu shu-watsu
gya-tei gya-tei, ha-ra gya-tei,
hara-sō gya-tei, bo-ji so-wa-ka
han-nya shin-gyō
---
So the mantra of the perfection of wisdom is spoken. It says:
*gate gate pāragate pārasaṃgate bodhi svāhā*
(gone, gone, gone beyond, gone wholly beyond: awakening, so be it!)
The Heart Sutra.
:::

::: note Translation notes
**色 *shiki*** stands for Sanskrit *rūpa*, “form” or “matter”. It is not “colour” here, although the same character means colour elsewhere in the sutra (色声香味触法).

**空 *kū*** (*śūnyatā*) does not mean that nothing exists. It means that nothing exists independently or with a fixed nature of its own.

**The mantra** is kept untranslated in East Asian practice. The Japanese reading *gyatei gyatei haragyatei harasōgyatei bojisowaka* is a sound-transcription of the Sanskrit.
:::
