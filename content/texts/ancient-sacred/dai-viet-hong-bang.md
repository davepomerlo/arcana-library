::: note Notice
This is a **national origin myth** recorded by Confucian court historians, not a folk tale taken down from a storyteller. The historians link the Vietnamese royal line to the Chinese culture hero Shennong (炎帝神農), and the 19th-century Nguyễn emperor Tự Đức even ordered these early reigns struck from the official history as unreliable. The story of the hundred eggs is still told in Vietnam today as the origin of the word *đồng bào* (同胞, "compatriots", literally "of the same womb").
:::

## Kinh Dương Vương

::: parallel 1
壬戌元年初，炎帝神農氏三世孫帝明，生帝宜。既而南巡至五嶺，接得婺僊女，生王。王聖智聰明，帝明奇之，欲使嗣位。王固讓其兄，不敢奉命。
---
Year one, *nhâm tuất*. In the beginning, Đế Minh, great-grandson of the Flame Emperor Shennong, fathered Đế Nghi. Later he travelled south to the Five Ranges, met an immortal maiden of Vụ, and fathered the king. The king was wise and brilliant, and Đế Minh, marvelling at him, wanted him to inherit the throne; but the king firmly yielded to his elder brother and would not accept.
:::

::: parallel 2
帝明於是立帝宜為嗣，治北方，封王為涇陽王，治南方，號赤鬼國。王娶洞庭君女，曰神龍，生貉龍君。
---
So Đế Minh made Đế Nghi his heir, to rule the north, and enfeoffed the king as Kinh Dương Vương, to rule the south, in a land called Xích Quỷ. The king married the daughter of the Lord of Lake Dongting, named Thần Long ("Divine Dragon"), and she bore Lạc Long Quân.
:::

## Lạc Long Quân and Âu Cơ

::: parallel 3
君娶帝來女，曰嫗姬。生百男〔俗傳生百卵〕，是為百粤之祖。
---
Lạc Long Quân married the daughter of Đế Lai, named Âu Cơ. She bore a hundred sons [popular tradition says she gave birth to a hundred eggs]; they were the ancestors of the Hundred Yue.
:::

::: parallel 4
一日謂姬曰：「我是龍種，儞是僊種。水火相尅，合併實難。」乃與之相別。分五十子從母歸山，五十子從父居南〔居南作歸南海〕。封其長為雄王，嗣君位。
---
One day he said to Âu Cơ: "I am of the dragon kind, and you are of the immortal kind. Water and fire overcome each other; it is truly hard for us to stay together." So they parted. Fifty sons followed their mother back to the mountains, and fifty followed their father to live in the south [another reading: to return to the Southern Sea]. He made the eldest the Hùng king, to succeed to the throne.
:::

## The historian's comment

::: parallel 5
史臣吳士連曰：天地開肇之時，有以氣化者，盤古氏是也。有氣化，然後有形化，莫非陰陽二氣也。
---
The historian Ngô Sĩ Liên comments: When heaven and earth first opened, there were beings transformed out of *qi* — Pangu was one. After transformation by *qi* came transformation by form; all of it is nothing but the two *qi* of yin and yang.
:::

::: note Words worth knowing
- **龍種 / 僊種 (*long chủng* / *tiên chủng*)**: "dragon kind" and "immortal kind". Vietnamese people still call themselves *con Rồng cháu Tiên*, "children of the Dragon, grandchildren of the Immortal".
- **水火相尅**: "water and fire overcome each other", a phrase from the theory of the five phases (五行). The dragon belongs to water, the mountain immortal to fire.
- **雄王 Hùng Vương**: the Hùng kings, said to have ruled the kingdom of **Văn Lang**. Their death-anniversary festival on the 10th day of the 3rd lunar month is a national holiday in Vietnam.
- **百粤 (*Bách Việt*)**: the "Hundred Yue", the many peoples of southern China and northern Vietnam in ancient times. The name *Việt* in *Việt Nam* comes from it.
:::
