## What happened

L/L Research grew out of a meditation and channelling group that **Don Elkins**, a physics professor and airline pilot who had researched UFO contact claims since the 1950s, began in Louisville in the 1960s and 1970s. **Carla L. Rueckert** joined him and became the group's main speaking channel; **Jim McCarty** joined in 1980.

On 15 January 1981, during a teaching session, Rueckert went into a full trance, which was unusual for her: in ordinary channelling she stayed conscious. A different voice gave its name as **Ra**. Over the next three years the three held **106 sessions**, the last on 15 March 1984. Each followed the same pattern:

- Rueckert lay in trance and spoke; she said afterwards that she remembered nothing;
- Elkins asked the questions, often long and technical ones, and decided the line of inquiry;
- McCarty managed the room, the ritual and the tape recorder, and transcribed the tapes.

The source said repeatedly that the trance put a strain on Rueckert's body, and the sessions were often cut short to protect her. They ended when Elkins' health failed. He died in November 1984. Rueckert continued channelling in the conscious mode until her death in 2015.

## What the sessions say

The material is a long dialogue, not a book with chapters, and its ideas come out piece by piece in answer to Elkins' questions. Its main claims, in summary:

- **The Law of One.** All things are one Creator knowing itself. Free will, love and light are the first "distortions" of that unity.
- **Densities.** Consciousness evolves through seven levels, or "densities", plus an eighth that begins a new octave. Humans are at the end of the third, the density of self-awareness and choice.
- **The choice.** The work of third density is to choose between *service to others* and *service to self*. Either path, followed far enough, carries a being onward at "harvest".
- **The harvest.** The sessions place the end of the present cycle in the early 21st century.
- **Ra.** The speaker describes itself as a "social memory complex", a group being of sixth density, who claims to have contacted ancient Egypt and to regret how its teaching there was distorted.
- **The archetypes.** Later sessions turn to a 22-card system of archetypes of mind, body and spirit, closely tied to the Major Arcana of the tarot.

## How to read it

Read as a primary source, the Ra Material is unusual in three ways:

- **It is a record of sessions, not a composed book.** Every answer can be dated to a session, and the questioner's role in shaping the material is visible throughout. Elkins' own interests, such as UFOs, pyramids and physics, set much of the agenda.
- **The editing is documented.** The first edition omitted some personal material, which was later restored. The full text can now be compared with the original transcripts and audio.
- **It deals openly with its own conditions.** The sessions spend a great deal of time on Rueckert's health, on the group's harmony, and on what the source called "psychic greeting". Readers interested in how channelling works in practice will find unusually detailed evidence here.

The material makes no testable predictions in the ordinary sense, and nothing in it has been independently confirmed. It belongs to a long tradition of question-and-answer channelling that runs from Allan Kardec's *Livre des Esprits* (see [the archive's extract](kardec-spirits-book-on-god.html)) through the Seth Material to the present.
