::: note About this text
Hippolyte Rivail, a Lyon-born teacher and author of school textbooks, began attending table-turning séances in Paris in 1855. He put prepared questions to the spirits through several mediums, sorted the answers by subject, and published them in 1857 under the name **Allan Kardec**, which he said a spirit had told him was his name in a former life. The first edition had 501 questions; the second edition of 1860, the one used ever since, has 1,019.

Kardec explains his layout in a footnote to question 1: the text in quotation marks after each question is the spirits' own answer, and his remarks are set apart from it. The same layout is kept here, with Kardec's comments in italics.

Spiritism became a mass movement in Brazil, where Kardec's books still sell in large numbers.
:::

## God and infinity

::: parallel 1
**1. Qu'est-ce que Dieu ?**

« Dieu est l'intelligence suprême, cause première de toutes choses. »
---
**1. What is God?**

"God is the supreme intelligence, the first cause of all things."
:::

::: parallel 2
**2. Que doit-on entendre par l'infini ?**

« Ce qui n'a ni commencement ni fin : l'inconnu ; tout ce qui est inconnu est infini. »
---
**2. What should we understand by the infinite?**

"That which has neither beginning nor end: the unknown. All that is unknown is infinite."
:::

::: parallel 3
**3. Pourrait-on dire que Dieu c'est l'infini ?**

« Définition incomplète. Pauvreté de la langue des hommes qui est insuffisante pour définir les choses qui sont au-dessus de leur intelligence. »

*Dieu est infini dans ses perfections, mais l'infini est une abstraction ; dire que Dieu est l'infini, c'est prendre l'attribut pour la chose même, et définir une chose qui n'est pas connue par une chose qui ne l'est pas davantage.*
---
**3. Could one say that God is the infinite?**

"An incomplete definition. It is the poverty of human language, which is not enough to define things that are above human understanding."

*God is infinite in his perfections, but the infinite is an abstraction. To say that God is the infinite is to take the attribute for the thing itself, and to define something unknown by something no better known.*
:::

## Proof of God's existence

::: parallel 4
**4. Où peut-on trouver la preuve de l'existence de Dieu ?**

« Dans un axiome que vous appliquez à vos sciences : il n'y a pas d'effet sans cause. Cherchez la cause de tout ce qui n'est pas l'œuvre de l'homme, et votre raison vous répondra. »
---
**4. Where can the proof of God's existence be found?**

"In an axiom you apply in your sciences: there is no effect without a cause. Look for the cause of everything that is not the work of human beings, and your reason will answer you."
:::

## The attributes of the divinity

::: parallel 10
**10. L'homme peut-il comprendre la nature intime de Dieu ?**

« Non ; c'est un sens qui lui manque. »
---
**10. Can a human being understand the inner nature of God?**

"No. It is a sense that he lacks."
:::

::: parallel 11
**11. Sera-t-il un jour donné à l'homme de comprendre le mystère de la Divinité ?**

« Quand son esprit ne sera plus obscurci par la matière et que, par sa perfection, il se sera rapproché de lui, alors il le verra et il le comprendra. »

*L'infériorité des facultés de l'homme ne lui permet pas de comprendre la nature intime de Dieu.*
---
**11. Will it one day be given to human beings to understand the mystery of the Divinity?**

"When the spirit is no longer clouded by matter and, by its perfection, has drawn near to him, then it will see him and understand him."

*The limits of human faculties do not allow us to understand the inner nature of God.*
:::

::: note Reading Kardec today
Kardec presented Spiritism as a philosophy built on observation, and the opening questions show it: God is reached by an argument from cause and effect, of the kind found in eighteenth-century natural theology, rather than by revelation. Later chapters cover the nature of spirits, reincarnation, the plurality of inhabited worlds, and moral law. Readers comparing channelled texts will notice how much of the later question-and-answer literature, including the Ra Material, follows the form Kardec set.
:::
