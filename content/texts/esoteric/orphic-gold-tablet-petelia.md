::: note About the gold tablets
About forty of these inscribed gold leaves are known. They come from graves in southern Italy, Sicily, Thessaly, Crete and Macedonia, and date from c. 400 BCE to the 2nd century CE. The longest and oldest is from Hipponion, in Calabria. They share a set of instructions: which spring to avoid, which to drink from, and what to say to the guardians or to Persephone. They are among the very few first-hand documents of Greek mystery religion.
:::

::: parallel 1–3
Εὑρήσεις δ᾽ Ἀΐδαο δόμων ἐπ᾽ ἀριστερὰ κρήνην,
πὰρ δ᾽ αὐτῆι λευκὴν ἑστηκυῖαν κυπάρισσον·
ταύτης τῆς κρήνης μηδὲ σχεδὸν ἐμπελάσειας.
---
You will find in the halls of Hades, on the left, a spring,
and standing beside it a white cypress.
Do not go anywhere near this spring.
:::

::: parallel 4–5
εὑρήσεις δ᾽ ἑτέραν, τῆς Μνημοσύνης ἀπὸ λίμνης
ψυχρὸν ὕδωρ προρέον· φύλακες δ᾽ ἐπίπροσθεν ἔασιν.
---
You will find another, cold water flowing forth
from the lake of Memory (Mnemosyne); and there are guardians before it.
:::

::: parallel 6–9
εἰπεῖν· Γῆς παῖς εἰμι καὶ Οὐρανοῦ ἀστερόεντος,
αὐτὰρ ἐμοὶ γένος οὐράνιον· τόδε δ᾽ ἴστε καὶ αὐτοί.
δίψηι δ᾽ εἰμὶ αὔη καὶ ἀπόλλυμαι· ἀλλὰ δότ᾽ αἶψα
ψυχρὸν ὕδωρ προρέον τῆς Μνημοσύνης ἀπὸ λίμνης.
---
Say: “I am a child of Earth and starry Heaven,
but my race is of Heaven; this you know yourselves.
I am parched with thirst and I am perishing. Quickly, give me
the cold water flowing forth from the lake of Memory.”
:::

::: parallel 10–11
καὐτ[οί σοι δώσουσι πιεῖν θείης ἀπὸ κρή]νης,
καὶ τότ᾽ ἔπειτ᾽ ἄ[λλοισι μεθ᾽] ἡρώεσσιν ἀνάξει[ς].
---
And they themselves will give you to drink from the divine spring,
and then you will rule among the other heroes.
:::

::: note Translation notes
**The spring on the left** is not named on this tablet. Ancient and modern readers take it to be Lethe, Forgetfulness, which makes the dead forget their past lives. Drinking from Memory instead preserves the initiate's knowledge and breaks the cycle of rebirth.

**“Child of Earth and starry Heaven”** echoes Hesiod's *Theogony*. The initiate claims kinship with the gods. Orphic myth explained this through humanity's descent from the Titans who devoured the infant Dionysus.
:::
