::: note Translation notes
**信心 *xìnxīn*** (Japanese *shinjin*) is translated “trust in mind”. It can mean both “faith in the mind” and “the mind that trusts”, and the poem's last lines turn on that ambiguity: 信心不二，不二信心. **揀擇 *jiǎnzé***, “picking and choosing”, is the poem's target: preference that splits experience into two.
:::

::: parallel 1
至道無難，唯嫌揀擇。
但莫憎愛，洞然明白。
毫釐有差，天地懸隔。
欲得現前，莫存順逆。
---
The Supreme Way is not difficult; it only refuses picking and choosing.
Simply have no hate or love, and it is wide open and clear.
But a hair's breadth of difference, and heaven and earth are set apart.
If you want it to appear before you, hold no views for or against.
:::

::: parallel 2
違順相爭，是為心病。
不識玄旨，徒勞念靜。
圓同太虛，無欠無餘。
良由取捨，所以不如。
---
Setting what you dislike against what you like: this is the sickness of the mind.
Not knowing the deep meaning, you labour in vain to still your thoughts.
It is round and complete like vast space, lacking nothing, with nothing extra.
It is only because we grasp and reject that we do not see it as it is.
:::

::: parallel 3
莫逐有緣，勿住空忍。
一種平懷，泯然自盡。
止動歸止，止更彌動。
唯滯兩邊，寧知一種。
---
Do not chase after conditioned things; do not dwell in empty acceptance.
In the oneness of an even mind, they vanish by themselves.
Stop movement to return to stillness, and the stillness moves all the more.
Stuck at either extreme, how can you know the oneness?
:::

::: parallel 4
一種不通，兩處失功。
遣有沒有，從空背空。
多言多慮，轉不相應。
絕言絕慮，無處不通。
---
Not penetrating the oneness, you fail in both places.
Push away being and you sink into it; follow emptiness and you turn your back on it.
The more talk and thought, the further you are from it.
Cut off talk and thought, and there is nowhere you cannot pass.
:::

::: parallel 5
歸根得旨，隨照失宗。
須臾返照，勝卻前空。
前空轉變，皆由妄見。
不用求真，唯須息見。
---
Return to the root and you find the meaning; chase the reflections and you lose the source.
One moment of turning the light around surpasses all the emptiness before you.
The changes in that emptiness before you all come from deluded views.
Do not seek the true; just stop holding views.
:::

::: parallel 6
二見不住，慎莫追尋。
纔有是非，紛然失心。
二由一有，一亦莫守。
一心不生，萬法無咎。
---
Do not stay in dualistic views; take care not to pursue them.
As soon as there is right and wrong, the mind is lost in confusion.
Two comes from one, but do not cling even to the one.
When the one mind does not arise, the ten thousand dharmas are without fault.
:::

::: parallel 7
無咎無法，不生不心。
能隨境滅，境逐能沈。
境由能境，能由境能。
欲知兩段，元是一空。
---
Without fault, there are no dharmas; with nothing arising, there is no mind.
The subject vanishes with its objects; the objects sink away with the subject.
Objects are objects because of the subject; the subject is a subject because of its objects.
If you want to know what the two are: originally they are one emptiness.
:::

::: parallel 8
一空同兩，齊含萬象。
不見精麁，寧有偏黨。
大道體寬，無易無難。
小見狐疑，轉急轉遲。
---
The one emptiness is the same as the two, and holds all the myriad forms equally.
If you do not see fine and coarse, how can you take sides?
The body of the Great Way is vast; it is neither easy nor difficult.
Narrow views are fox-like doubts: the more you hurry, the slower you go.
:::

::: parallel 9
執之失度，必入邪路。
放之自然，體無去住。
任性合道，逍遙絕惱。
繫念乖真，昏沈不好。
---
Grasp at it and you lose your measure, and surely go down a crooked road.
Let it go and be natural; its essence neither goes nor stays.
Trust your nature and accord with the Way, wandering at ease, free of vexation.
Tie down your thoughts and you stray from the true, sinking into dullness, which is no good.
:::

::: parallel 10
不好勞神，何用疏親。
欲取一乘，勿惡六塵。
六塵不惡，還同正覺。
智者無為，愚人自縛。
---
It is no good to weary the spirit. Why keep some things at a distance and hold others close?
If you wish to take the One Vehicle, do not dislike the six sense-objects.
Not disliking the six sense-objects is itself the same as true awakening.
The wise do not act; the foolish tie themselves up.
:::

::: parallel 11
法無異法，妄自愛著。
將心用心，豈非大錯。
迷生寂亂，悟無好惡。
一切二邊，良由斟酌。
---
Dharmas are not different from one another; we deludedly fall in love with them and cling.
To use the mind to work on the mind: is this not a great mistake?
Confusion gives rise to calm and disturbance; awakening has no likes or dislikes.
All the pairs of opposites come only from weighing and deliberating.
:::

::: parallel 12
夢幻空花，何勞把捉。
得失是非，一時放卻。
眼若不睡，諸夢自除。
心若不異，萬法一如。
---
Dreams, illusions, flowers in the sky: why struggle to grasp them?
Gain and loss, right and wrong: let them go all at once.
If the eye does not sleep, all dreams cease by themselves.
If the mind does not differentiate, the ten thousand dharmas are one suchness.
:::

::: parallel 13
一如體玄，兀爾忘緣。
萬法齊觀，歸復自然。
泯其所以，不可方比。
止動無動，動止無止。
---
The essence of the one suchness is profound; unmoving, it forgets all conditions.
When the ten thousand dharmas are seen as equal, they return to what they naturally are.
When the reasons for things vanish, there is nothing to compare them with.
Stop movement and there is no movement; set stillness moving and there is no stillness.
:::

::: parallel 14
兩既不成，一何有爾。
究竟窮極，不存軌則。
契心平等，所作俱息。
狐疑盡淨，正信調直。
---
When two cannot be established, how can there be one?
At the ultimate end, no rules or standards remain.
When the mind is in accord with equality, all doing comes to rest.
Fox-like doubts are cleared away completely, and true trust is set straight.
:::

::: parallel 15
一切不留，無可記憶。
虛明自照，不勞心力。
非思量處，識情難測。
真如法界，無他無自。
---
Nothing lingers; there is nothing to remember.
Empty and bright, it illuminates itself, with no effort of mind.
This is a place beyond thought, which consciousness and feeling can hardly fathom.
In the Dharma-realm of true suchness there is neither other nor self.
:::

::: parallel 16
要急相應，唯言不二。
不二皆同，無不包容。
十方智者，皆入此宗。
宗非促延，一念萬年。
---
If you urgently want to accord with it, just say “not two”.
In “not two” all things are the same; there is nothing it does not include.
The wise of the ten directions all enter this teaching.
This teaching is neither short nor long: one thought is ten thousand years.
:::

::: parallel 17
無在不在，十方目前。
極小同大，忘絕境界。
極大同小，不見邊表。
有即是無，無即是有。
---
Not here, not not here: the ten directions are right before your eyes.
The smallest is the same as the largest, when boundaries are forgotten.
The largest is the same as the smallest; you do not see its edges.
Being is non-being; non-being is being.
:::

::: parallel 18
若不如是，必不須守。
一即一切，一切即一。
但能如是，何慮不畢。
信心不二，不二信心。
言語道斷，非去來今。
---
If it is not like this, you must certainly not hold on to it.
One is all; all is one.
If only you can be like this, why worry about not finishing?
Trusting mind is not two; not two is trusting mind.
Here the way of words is cut off: not past, not future, not present.
:::
