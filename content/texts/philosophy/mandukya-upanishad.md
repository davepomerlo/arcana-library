::: parallel 1
ओमित्येतदक्षरमिदं सर्वं तस्योपव्याख्यानं भूतं भवद्भविष्यदिति सर्वमोङ्कार एव ।
यच्चान्यत्त्रिकालातीतं तदप्योङ्कार एव ॥
---
om ity etad akṣaram idaṃ sarvaṃ tasyopavyākhyānaṃ bhūtaṃ bhavad bhaviṣyad iti sarvam oṃkāra eva |
yac cānyat trikālātītaṃ tad apy oṃkāra eva ||
---
Om: this syllable is all this. Here is its explanation. What was, what is and what will be: all of it is just Om.
And whatever else lies beyond the three times, that too is just Om.
:::

::: parallel 2
सर्वं ह्येतद् ब्रह्मायमात्मा ब्रह्म सोऽयमात्मा चतुष्पात् ॥
---
sarvaṃ hy etad brahmāyam ātmā brahma so 'yam ātmā catuṣpāt ||
---
For all this is *brahman*. This self is *brahman*. This self has four quarters.
:::

::: parallel 3
जागरितस्थानो बहिष्प्रज्ञः सप्ताङ्ग एकोनविंशतिमुखः स्थूलभुग्वैश्वानरः प्रथमः पादः ॥
---
jāgaritasthāno bahiṣprajñaḥ saptāṅga ekonaviṃśatimukhaḥ sthūlabhug vaiśvānaraḥ prathamaḥ pādaḥ ||
---
The first quarter is Vaiśvānara, “common to all people”. Its field is the waking state. It is aware of what is outside, has seven limbs and nineteen mouths, and enjoys gross things.
:::

::: parallel 4
स्वप्नस्थानोऽन्तःप्रज्ञः सप्ताङ्ग एकोनविंशतिमुखः प्रविविक्तभुक्तैजसो द्वितीयः पादः ॥
---
svapnasthāno 'ntaḥprajñaḥ saptāṅga ekonaviṃśatimukhaḥ praviviktabhuk taijaso dvitīyaḥ pādaḥ ||
---
The second quarter is Taijasa, “the luminous”. Its field is dream. It is aware of what is inside, has seven limbs and nineteen mouths, and enjoys subtle things.
:::

::: parallel 5
यत्र सुप्तो न कञ्चन कामं कामयते न कञ्चन स्वप्नं पश्यति तत् सुषुप्तम् ।
सुषुप्तस्थान एकीभूतः प्रज्ञानघन एवानन्दमयो ह्यानन्दभुक् चेतोमुखः प्राज्ञस्तृतीयः पादः ॥
---
yatra supto na kañcana kāmaṃ kāmayate na kañcana svapnaṃ paśyati tat suṣuptam |
suṣuptasthāna ekībhūtaḥ prajñānaghana evānandamayo hy ānandabhuk cetomukhaḥ prājñas tṛtīyaḥ pādaḥ ||
---
When a sleeper desires nothing and sees no dream, that is deep sleep.
The third quarter is Prājña, “the knower”. Its field is deep sleep. It has become one, a single mass of awareness, made of bliss and enjoying bliss, and thought is its mouth.
:::

::: parallel 6
एष सर्वेश्वर एष सर्वज्ञ एषोऽन्तर्याम्येष योनिः सर्वस्य प्रभवाप्ययौ हि भूतानाम् ॥
---
eṣa sarveśvara eṣa sarvajña eṣo 'ntaryāmy eṣa yoniḥ sarvasya prabhavāpyayau hi bhūtānām ||
---
This is the lord of all; this is the knower of all; this is the inner controller; this is the womb of all, for it is the origin and the dissolution of beings.
:::

::: parallel 7
नान्तःप्रज्ञं न बहिष्प्रज्ञं नोभयतःप्रज्ञं न प्रज्ञानघनं न प्रज्ञं नाप्रज्ञम् ।
अदृष्टमव्यवहार्यमग्राह्यमलक्षणमचिन्त्यमव्यपदेश्यमेकात्मप्रत्ययसारं प्रपञ्चोपशमं शान्तं शिवमद्वैतं चतुर्थं मन्यन्ते स आत्मा स विज्ञेयः ॥
---
nāntaḥprajñaṃ na bahiṣprajñaṃ nobhayataḥprajñaṃ na prajñānaghanaṃ na prajñaṃ nāprajñam |
adṛṣṭam avyavahāryam agrāhyam alakṣaṇam acintyam avyapadeśyam ekātmapratyayasāraṃ prapañcopaśamaṃ śāntaṃ śivam advaitaṃ caturthaṃ manyante sa ātmā sa vijñeyaḥ ||
---
Not aware of the inside, not aware of the outside, not aware of both, not a mass of awareness, not aware, not unaware;
unseen, beyond dealings, ungraspable, without marks, unthinkable, indescribable, whose essence is the certainty of the one self, the stilling of the world's spreading-out (*prapañca*), peaceful, auspicious, non-dual: this they consider the fourth. That is the self; that is what is to be known.
:::

::: parallel 8
सोऽयमात्माऽध्यक्षरमोङ्कारोऽधिमात्रं पादा मात्रा मात्राश्च पादा अकार उकारो मकार इति ॥
---
so 'yam ātmādhyakṣaram oṃkāro 'dhimātraṃ pādā mātrā mātrāś ca pādā akāra ukāro makāra iti ||
---
This self, in terms of syllables, is Om; in terms of its parts, the quarters are the parts and the parts are the quarters: the sounds A, U and M.
:::

::: parallel 9
जागरितस्थानो वैश्वानरोऽकारः प्रथमा मात्राऽऽप्तेरादिमत्त्वाद्वाऽऽप्नोति ह वै सर्वान्कामानादिश्च भवति य एवं वेद ॥
---
jāgaritasthāno vaiśvānaro 'kāraḥ prathamā mātrāpter ādimattvād vāpnoti ha vai sarvān kāmān ādiś ca bhavati ya evaṃ veda ||
---
Vaiśvānara, whose field is waking, is A, the first sound, because it pervades (*āpti*) or because it comes first (*ādimattva*). Whoever knows this obtains all desires and becomes first.
:::

::: parallel 10
स्वप्नस्थानस्तैजस उकारो द्वितीया मात्रोत्कर्षात् उभयत्वाद्वोत्कर्षति ह वै ज्ञानसन्ततिं समानश्च भवति नास्याब्रह्मवित्कुले भवति य एवं वेद ॥
---
svapnasthānas taijasa ukāro dvitīyā mātrotkarṣād ubhayatvād votkarṣati ha vai jñānasantatiṃ samānaś ca bhavati nāsyābrahmavit kule bhavati ya evaṃ veda ||
---
Taijasa, whose field is dream, is U, the second sound, because it is higher (*utkarṣa*) or because it lies between the two. Whoever knows this raises the stream of knowledge and becomes balanced; in his family no one is born who does not know *brahman*.
:::

::: parallel 11
सुषुप्तस्थानः प्राज्ञो मकारस्तृतीया मात्रा मितेरपीतेर्वा मिनोति ह वा इदं सर्वमपीतिश्च भवति य एवं वेद ॥
---
suṣuptasthānaḥ prājño makāras tṛtīyā mātrā miter apīter vā minoti ha vā idaṃ sarvam apītiś ca bhavati ya evaṃ veda ||
---
Prājña, whose field is deep sleep, is M, the third sound, because it measures (*miti*) or because everything merges into it (*apīti*). Whoever knows this measures all this and becomes its merging-place.
:::

::: parallel 12
अमात्रश्चतुर्थोऽव्यवहार्यः प्रपञ्चोपशमः शिवोऽद्वैत एवमोङ्कार आत्मैव संविशत्यात्मनाऽऽत्मानं य एवं वेद ॥
---
amātraś caturtho 'vyavahāryaḥ prapañcopaśamaḥ śivo 'dvaita evam oṃkāra ātmaiva saṃviśaty ātmanātmānaṃ ya evaṃ veda ||
---
The fourth has no parts. It is beyond dealings, the stilling of the world's spreading-out, auspicious, non-dual. So Om is the self itself. Whoever knows this enters the self by the self.
:::

::: note Translation notes
**तुरीय *turīya*** (“the fourth”) is not named as such in the Upanishad, which says only *caturtha*, “fourth”. The name *turīya* comes from Gauḍapāda's Kārikā and later Vedānta.

**The puns in verses 9–11** link each sound to a Sanskrit word that begins with it: A to *āpti* and *ādi*, U to *utkarṣa* and *ubhaya*, M to *miti* and *apīti*. A translation can only keep them by giving the Sanskrit in brackets.
:::
